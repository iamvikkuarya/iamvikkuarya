V. Method / Loop • 04 stages, iterative 005 / 008
Method · Nº 05

How I actually work.

+

Every stage is iterative, evidence-driven, and documented - composable tests, not opaque scripts.

01

Detect →

Scan the surface: enumerate endpoints, map the attack surface, identify the test boundaries and environment constraints.

02

Discover →

Turn 1 is the threat model: surface, audience, risk, OWASP category, data exposure. Locked down before a single test runs.

03

Direct →

Author the test suite: framework architecture, parallel execution, isolation. Burp Suite and pytest pick the deterministic path.

04

Deliver

The audit reports to disk: findings, impact analysis, remediation aligned to OWASP. You ship the framework and the evidence together.

Tests inform everything. Reports make it real.
